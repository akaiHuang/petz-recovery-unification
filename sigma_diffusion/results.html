Key Result

After step 350 (35% of training), Σ drops to < 1% of peak. The Petz fidelity bound F ≥ exp(-Σ/2) = 0.9999 at late stages — consecutive states are 99.99% recoverable. Loss still improves (0.14→0.09) but the information-theoretic structure is fully set. Σ(t) ≈ 0.0068·exp(-0.00454t) with R²>0.95.

The Universal Pattern

Burst → Coast → Cleanup

All three experiments show the same Σ profile: a burst of high information change early, a long coasting phase with near-zero Σ, and sometimes a small final refinement. This is predicted by the Petz recovery bound: F ≥ exp(-Σ/2) means low-Σ steps are nearly reversible — they can be skipped without information loss.

Why Σ-guided beats uniform

Uniform step reduction (DDIM) treats all time regions equally. Σ-guided allocates compute to high-information regions and skips low-information regions. At 20 steps on SD v1.5: +0.38 dB. At 64 steps on MDLM: -37 PPL. Same principle, different domains, consistent improvement.
