## 2. Division Algebras and Generations

### 2.1 Fermion Mass Ratios from J_3(O) -- KEY PAPER

- **Tejinder P. Singh** (2025), "Fermion mass ratios from the exceptional Jordan algebra", arXiv:2508.10131
