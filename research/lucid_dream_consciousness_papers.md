# Lucid Dreaming & Consciousness - Key Papers
## 2026-03-19

## Brain Waves in Lucid Dreams
- 40Hz gamma waves induce lucid dreams (Nature Neuroscience, 77% success rate)
- Frontal cortex gamma reactivation during lucid REM
- Alpha connectivity increased in lucid state
- Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/nn.3719

## Time Perception in Dreams
- Motor tasks take ~40% longer in lucid dreams than waking
- Dream time is elastic, task-dependent
- Source: PMC3893623

## Dream Memory Loss (Active Forgetting)
- MCH neurons actively prevent memory storage during REM (NIH 2019)
- Must wake directly from REM to retain; other sleep stages erase
- Norepinephrine absent during REM (needed for memory consolidation)
- Hippocampus deactivated during REM
- Source: NIH news, PMC2814941
