## 1. Modified Gravity GW Constraints

### 1.1 GWTC-4 and O4 Results (March 2026)

- **LVK Collaboration**, "GWTC-4.0: Tests of General Relativity. I. Overview and General Tests," arXiv:2603.19019 (March 2026).
  Suite of GR tests using 91 confident signals (including 42 new O4a events). No statistically significant deviations from GR found across all tests. The most comprehensive GR test battery to date.

- **LVK Collaboration**, "GWTC-4.0: Tests of General Relativity. II. Parameterized Tests," arXiv:2603.19020 (March 2026).
  Parameterized post-Einsteinian tests with doubled event count relative to GWTC-3, tightening constraints on beyond-GR parameters.

- **LVK Collaboration**, "GWTC-4.0: Tests of General Relativity. III. Tests of the Remnants," arXiv:2603.19021 (March 2026).
  Seven tests of merger remnant properties. All consistent with Kerr black hole predictions.

- **LVK Collaboration**, "Black Hole Spectroscopy and Tests of General Relativity with GW250114" (February 2026).
  First detection of three quasinormal mode tones in a single event. Most stringent single-event GR verification ever performed; constraints 2-3x better than combining dozens of previous events. Confirms Kerr nature of black holes.

### 1.2 GW Speed and Modified Gravity

- **Post-GW170817 landscape**: The $c_T = c$ constraint ($|c_T/c - 1| < \text{few} \times 10^{-15}$) remains the single most devastating constraint on modified gravity. **Our framework is structurally safe**: $c_T = c$ exactly in the Khronon approach because the tensor sector is unmodified.

- Karmakar & Haridasu, "Dynamical Dark Energy or Modified Gravity? Signatures in Gravitational Wave Propagation," arXiv:2509.07976 (September 2025).
  Shows modified gravity theories produce distinctive signatures via two parameters: effective gravitational coupling and GW amplitude damping. DESI BAO + SNe data used to reconstruct dark energy within $f(Q)$ gravity. Next-gen detectors (LISA, ET) can distinguish models. **Relevant**: our $\Sigma$ framework predicts specific amplitude damping behavior.

- Lin, Zhu, Niu & Zhao, "Constraining the modified friction in gravitational wave propagation with precessing black hole binaries," arXiv:2404.11245 (April 2024).
  Using precessing BBH events, constrains modified friction parameter $\Xi_0 = 1.002^{+0.004}_{-0.004}$, two orders of magnitude improvement over previous analyses. **Relevant**: $\Sigma$ predicts no GW friction modification ($\Xi_0 = 1$ exactly) because tensor modes propagate on the GR light cone.

### 1.3 Modified Dispersion Relations and Lorentz Violation

- Wang, Yan, Zhu & Zhao, "Modified gravitational wave propagations in linearized gravity with Lorentz and diffeomorphism violations and their gravitational wave constraints," arXiv:2501.11956 (January 2025).
  Systematic analysis within the Standard Model Extension (SME) framework. Lorentz/diffeomorphism violations modify GW dispersion, producing anisotropy, birefringence, and dispersion. Analysis of GWTC events finds no evidence; 90% confidence bounds on all violating coefficients established.

- Baka, Cirok, Haris, Noller & Krishnendu, "Testing general relativity with gravitational waves -- improving and extending Modified Dispersion Relation tests," arXiv:2511.00497 (November 2025).
  Improved methodology yields ~19% tighter posteriors. Updated graviton mass bound: $m_g < 2.21 \times 10^{-11}$ peV. No evidence for LIV at any exponent tested.

- Wang, Yang & Han, "Tests of gravitational wave propagation with LIGO-Virgo catalog," arXiv:2404.14684 (April 2024).
  Tests massive gravity and WEP violations using BBH mergers from GWTC-2.1 and GWTC-3. No evidence for deviations found.

### 1.4 Scalar-Tensor Theory Constraints

- Wang & Yang, "Constraining scalar-tensor theories from higher harmonics with GW230529," arXiv:2505.15448 (May 2025).
  GW230529 (likely NSBH) used to test screened modified gravity and Brans-Dicke theory. Higher harmonics improve constraints by 13-30%. Combined multi-event analysis gives strongest GW constraint on Brans-Dicke: $\omega_{BD} > 123.75$.

- de Kruijf, Galloni & Bartolo, "The first data-driven bounds on the quantum decoherence of inflationary gravitational waves," arXiv:2511.14727 (November 2025).
  First observational bounds on quantum decoherence of primordial GWs using Planck + BICEP/Keck + LVK + BBN + PTA data. Some decoherence scenarios align with PTA signals. **Relevant**: these bounds constrain the rate at which $\tau \to 1$ in the early universe.

- arXiv:2501.18111 (October 2025): GW bursts decohere quantum superpositions. Individual contributions from memory and oscillatory components identified.

- Cang & Wang, "Universality and Falsifiability of Quantum Spacetime Decoherence: A Gauge-Invariant Framework for Gravitational-Wave Phase Diffusion," arXiv:2512.02782 (December 2025).
  Phase diffusion is the unique leading-order imprint of microscopic curvature fluctuations. Phase variance grows linearly with distance (universal). Frequency exponent discriminates between string foam, holographic noise, and causal-set models. Testable by LIGO, LISA, and PTAs. **Highly relevant**: in our framework, phase diffusion = information loss along the channel, directly quantifiable via $\Sigma$.

- **Construction begins 2026**, target first observations ~2035.
- Up to 8x more sensitive than current detectors across the band.
- Extended frequency band below 10 Hz.
- Science goals include: testing GR near horizons, probing quantum gravity, understanding DM nature, testing Lorentz invariance and alternative gravity at cosmological scales.

- Operational target: mid-2030s to 2040s.
