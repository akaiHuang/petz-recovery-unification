# Lucid Dream Detailed Research
## 2026-03-19

## 1. Time Perception
- Motor tasks 40% slower in lucid dreams (Frontiers Psychology)
- Lucid dreamers CAN manipulate perceived time flow
- Prefrontal cortex deactivation → temporal distortion
- Source: PMC3893623

## 2. Spatial Constraints
- Vestibular system absent during REM → spatial navigation impaired
- Large-scale environment changes destabilize dreams
- Dream space NOT completely unconstrained
- Source: PMC10779452, PMC12384728

## 3. Information Retention
- Keeping eyes closed = preventing startle response = maintaining hypnagogic state
- Must record immediately upon waking before any other activity
- First-person present tense enhances recall
- Source: PMC10078162

## 4. Entropy in Dream States
- LZC ordering: Wake > REM ≥ Light sleep >> Deep sleep
- Lucid dreaming: intermediate between REM and wake
- LZC alone does NOT track dream experience well
- Source: Frontiers fnhum.2022.987714

## 5. Time Reversal / Retrocausality
- 17.8-38% people report precognitive dreams (selection bias)
- No direct neuroscience evidence for retrocausality
- Dreams = probabilistic future simulations, not predictions
- Petz retrodiction framework provides mathematical analogy

## 6. Quantum Effects in Sleep
- Spin-entanglement signal correlates with conscious state (wake vs sleep) - 2024
- Epothilone B (microtubule stabilizer) delays anesthesia >60s
- Sleep-wake transitions may involve coherence-decoherence cycles
